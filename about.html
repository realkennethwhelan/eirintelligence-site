About Eirintelligence Global

A U.S. semiconductor solutions company built for global industry

Eirintelligence Global, Inc. connects U.S. semiconductor manufacturers with industrial customers worldwide — starting in Europe — through distribution, technical support, and supply chain services.

Our Story

Who we are

Eirintelligence Global, Inc. was incorporated in the United States in 2025 with a clear mission: to build a technology-driven bridge between American semiconductor manufacturers and industrial customers around the world.

We are more than a reseller. As a semiconductor solutions provider, we combine component sourcing with technical support and supply chain services — helping customers select the right parts, not just quote a price.

Our founder manages supplier relationships, customer development, and business operations directly, giving customers a responsive, technically grounded point of contact throughout the buying journey.

"Our suppliers are U.S. semiconductor manufacturers. Being established in the United States lets us work directly with them — while serving industrial customers wherever they are."

Our Journey

How we got here

2025 — Preparation

Eirintelligence Global Founded

Incorporated in the United States. Focused on market research, identifying U.S. semiconductor suppliers, and developing the business model and go-to-market strategy.

2026 — Business Validation

Operations & First Transactions

Capital deployed into operations. Established relationships with U.S. semiconductor manufacturers, obtained quotations, issued purchase orders, and began developing European customers.

2027 — Expansion

Customer & Team Growth

Growing the European customer base, expanding supplier relationships, and beginning to build a U.S.-based team as transaction volume increases.

2028 & Beyond

Platform Growth

Building a broader semiconductor solutions platform — expanding technical support capability and developing customers beyond Europe.
